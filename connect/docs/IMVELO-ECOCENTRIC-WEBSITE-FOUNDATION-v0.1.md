# imveloecocentric.org.za — Independent NPC Website Foundation v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-09. **Status:** Founder-side exploratory blueprint; not NPC board approval, domain/DNS verification or authorisation to publish.

## Purpose / organisational distinction

The website represents **Imvelo Eco-Centric NPC** as its own legal and community-facing entity: mission, ECD and local capability development, environmental focus, programmes and partnerships. **Imvelo Eco-Centric NPC is legally distinct from IGV.** DigiZone4All is an exploratory branded vehicle that Imvelo may use through an explicitly agreed relationship; the NPC does not automatically own DigiZone or IGV underlying IP.

Do not assume earlier materials describing Imvelo as an IGV subsidiary reflect current legal ownership or agreed contractual position. Verify any public governance, director, registration, NPO/PBO or tax-exemption claims against official records before publishing.

## Suggested site structure

| Page | Purpose | Launch content needed |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Explain the organisation and whom it serves | Approved short description, hero, 1–2 clear participation paths |
| About Imvelo | Identity and history | Legal name, registration status/number if approved, mission, leadership info if approved |
| Our Work | ECD, communities, environmental focus | Current genuinely active programmes separated from proposals |
| Community Stories | Outcomes supported by evidence | Real stories only with publication permission; placeholders otherwise |
| Work With Us | Partnerships/participation | Approved eligibility, contact, intended collaboration model |
| Contact | Reach the actual NPC | Approved contact channel and responsible person |

## Relationship with DigiZone4All

Describe DigiZone publicly only after Human approval of correct licence/implementation/brand attribution and NPC-specific decision. Suggested *private draft* framing: "Imvelo explores appropriate digital tools and capabilities for community organisations, including the DigiZone4All concept." Do **not** assert that DigiZone is an established NPC programme or that IGV owns/controls Imvelo.

## Visual identity

Imvelo's own identity and imagery should be developed/confirmed separately. DigiZone v0.1 visuals can be displayed where a DigiZone component is being demonstrated, but the overall NPC website should not automatically use the DigiZone nameplate as the NPC's logo.

## Readiness checklist (before any public NPC domain deployment)

- Human/organisational review of official mission, current work and contact information.
- Verification of registered legal details and exact use of NPC/NPO/PBO labels.
- Approval for public partner/beneficiary names, photography and stories.
- Explicit IP, collaboration, brand-use and data responsibility boundaries where DigiZone appears.
- Confirm domain registrar, DNS, hosting access, current site, redirects and email obligations.
- Mobile and accessibility testing, contact working, ownership/maintenance/recovery plan.

## Rollout

First prepare copy and low-risk private concept preview; separate later authorisation for public launch, DNS change and public representations. Avoid inventing impact metrics or donors.
